# Problem Statement

Research teams often manage projects through a mix of Excel trackers, email threads, Teams messages, meeting notes, and AI-agent outputs. The information exists, but it is distributed across tools and people.

In this setting, an AI agent can help summarize or analyze research materials, but it is not enough to manage the process. Without workflow orchestration, the agent does not reliably know deadlines, trigger approvals, remind owners, update a shared tracker, or produce auditable status reports.

## Core Problem

The research process needs a coordination layer that can:

- Capture incoming research tasks in a structured format
- Validate missing or inconsistent fields
- Assign clear ownership
- Route approvals when needed
- Track deadlines and send reminders
- Generate transparent progress reports
- Maintain a simple audit trail for stakeholders

## Target Users

- Research assistants
- Project supervisors
- Data science students
- Research teams using Microsoft 365 tools
- Teams experimenting with AI-agent support for analysis and reporting
